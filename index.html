Origin story

Radio instincts. Video muscle. Marketing discipline.

Megha began in radio and audio-video production, working behind the scenes and behind the mic. In 2020, she moved fully into digital content — blending sound, storytelling, and visuals into conversion-driven videos for YouTube, LinkedIn, and Instagram.

Her work lives at the intersection of creative direction and measurable growth: explainers, testimonials, reels, leadership bytes, product showcases, industry insights, employee advocacy, and full-funnel campaign planning.
